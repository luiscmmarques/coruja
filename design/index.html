Icons

icon.svg is the favicon and the any PWA icon — transparent, colour baked in. icon-maskable.svg is full-bleed for Android, where transparency would expose the launcher's own backdrop.

Check the browser tab. This page uses the real icon.svg as its favicon. Indigo is correct; black would mean currentColor has leaked into a file that gets loaded as an image.

Colour

One accent, a warm off-white ground, and three states. Contrast figures are against --bg and are the floor, not the target — this app is used by children in bad light and by adults at the end of the day.

Two golds, on purpose. --gold-fill is a badge or a medal, and never carries text. --gold is the text-safe version for an award label. Using the bright one for words would fail contrast at exactly the moment a child is being told they achieved something.
